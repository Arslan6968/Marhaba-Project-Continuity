# Decision Log

> Record only consequential decisions that future maintainers are likely to need. Avoid logging every small implementation choice. Never include secrets or private user data.

## [YYYY-MM-DD] — [Short decision title]

- **Status:** Proposed / Accepted / Superseded
- **Context:** What prompted the decision?
- **Decision:** What was chosen?
- **Reasoning:** Why this option?
- **Alternatives considered:** What reasonable alternatives were examined?
- **Consequences:** Trade-offs, limitations, or follow-up work.
- **Evidence / references:** Tests, measurements, issue IDs, or documents, where appropriate.
